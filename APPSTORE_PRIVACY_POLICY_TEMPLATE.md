# Privacy Policy - CarWash Booking

Last updated: [DATE]

CarWash Booking ("we", "our", "us") provides mobile booking services for car wash customers.

## Data we collect

- Phone number (for authentication and account access)
- Telegram username and Telegram ID (only when you authenticate via Telegram)
- Booking data (selected services, selected date/time, car details)
- Technical logs needed for security and error monitoring

## How we use data

- To authenticate users and protect account access
- To create, update, and manage bookings
- To provide customer support
- To maintain app security and prevent abuse

## Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data can be shared only:

- with infrastructure providers required to operate the service (hosting, database)
- when required by law

## Data retention

We store data only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations.
