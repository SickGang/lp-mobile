## Common issues

### I cannot log in via Telegram

1. Open login screen and enter your phone number.
2. Tap "Authorize via Telegram".
3. In Telegram bot, tap Start.
4. Return to the app and enter the received code.

### I do not receive the code

- Make sure your Telegram account is active.
- Re-open Telegram from the app and tap Start again.
- Check that the entered phone number matches your account.

Please include the phone number used for account registration.
